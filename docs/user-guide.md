# CareerPivots — User Guide

CareerPivots helps recently unemployed federal workers find private-sector jobs that match their experience. Upload your resume, and the app will analyze your background and surface the most relevant job matches — along with explanations of why each role fits your skills.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1 — Add your resume

In the left sidebar, you have two options:

**Option A — Upload a file**
Click "Upload a file" and select your resume in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format (maximum 5 MB).

**Option B — Paste your text**
Copy your resume text and paste it into the text box below the file uploader.

> If you provide both, the uploaded file takes priority.

---

### Step 2 — (Optional) Set your preferences

Two text boxes in the sidebar let you guide the results:

- **Preferences** — Describe what you're looking for. For example: *"Remote work, mission-driven organizations, opportunities for leadership"*
- **Exclusions** — Describe what you want to avoid. For example: *"On-call roles, startups under 10 people, heavy travel"*

These are free-text fields — write whatever is most useful to you.

---

### Step 3 — Choose how many results to see

Under **Results** in the sidebar, click one of the count buttons: **5**, **10**, **15**, or **20**. This controls how many job matches are returned.

---

### Step 4 — Find your matches

Click the **Find Matches** button. The app will:

1. Parse your resume into structured work experiences
2. Generate an embedding (a mathematical representation of your background)
3. Compare it against thousands of job profiles
4. Return the top matches, ranked by similarity

This typically takes 10–30 seconds depending on resume length.

---

## Reading Your Results

Each job match card shows:

**Match Score** — A percentage in the top-right corner of the card. Higher is better. A score above 80% indicates a strong alignment.

**Job Title & Description** — What the role involves.

**Why this matches your experience** — A short AI-generated explanation connecting your specific background to this role. If this section is loading slowly, the app is waiting on the AI service — it will appear automatically.

**Job Insights** — Four statistics about this role in the broader job market:
- *Database %* — How common this job type is
- *Frequency Rank* — Its rank by frequency (lower number = more common)
- *Avg Tenure* — How long people typically stay in this role
- *Median Tenure* — The midpoint tenure across all people in this role

**Top Career Transitions** — The three most common jobs people move into from this role, with percentage bars showing how frequently each transition occurs.

---

## Exporting Your Matches

You can download a formatted report of selected matches:

1. Check the **Export** checkbox on any job card you want to include
2. Use **Select all** (top right of the results list) to select everything at once
3. When at least one match is selected, a **Download (HTML)** button appears — click it to save your report
4. Open the downloaded `.html` file in any web browser to read or print it

The report includes the job title, match score, description, AI explanation, and all four job insights for each selected match.

## Interest Profile (Optional)

The **Interest Profile** section in the sidebar can improve your match quality by incorporating your vocational interests.

Click **Take O*NET Interest Questionnaire** to open a 30-question wizard. Each question describes a work activity — rate how you'd feel about doing it on a scale from *Strongly Dislike* to *Strongly Like*.

The questionnaire takes about 5 minutes. When complete, your RIASEC scores (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional) will appear in the sidebar. These scores reflect your natural work interests and help the system surface roles that align with both your experience and your preferences.

You can retake the questionnaire at any time by clicking the button again.

## Tips for Better Results

- **More detail = better matches.** A resume with specific bullet points and skills will produce more accurate results than a brief summary.
- **Use your full resume.** Don't trim it down — the more context the system has, the better.
- **Try different result counts.** Sometimes increasing to 15 or 20 reveals strong matches that didn't appear in the top 10.
- **Read the explanations.** The "Why this matches" section often surfaces connections you might not have considered.
- **Use the Interest Profile.** Even if you're confident in your direction, the RIASEC scores can reveal adjacent roles worth exploring.

---

## Tabs

The app has three main tabs at the top:

- **Resume Matching** — The main job matching experience described above
- **Job Market Statistics** — Browse statistics about job frequency, tenure, industry distribution, and growth rates across the entire database
- **Analytics Dashboard** — Visualize career transition flows using Sankey diagrams and explore filtered transition patterns
